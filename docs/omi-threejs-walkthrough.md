# Omi concept walkthrough — recording brief

Target length: **45–60 seconds**. Record the deployed desktop experience at 1440×900 or larger with the browser chrome hidden.

## Shot order

| Time | Action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Hold on Frame 1 before touching anything. | Establish the product and full-width Omi wordmark. |
| 4–10s | Hover the pendant to wake the LED, then move across it once to release a subtle swing. | Demonstrate material response, lighting, and weighted physics. |
| 10–22s | Scroll into Frame 2. Let the stagger finish, click the film, play two seconds, then close it. | Show that the story combines product explanation with real media interaction. |
| 22–34s | Scroll into Frame 3 and hold while the conveyor moves through the scene. | Reveal the product system, memory proof panel, and instanced hardware sequence. |
| 34–45s | Scroll into Frame 4, pause on the wearable image and CTA, then open “See what’s included.” | Resolve the narrative into product confidence and purchase intent. |
| 45–50s | End on the live URL or title card: “Omi hardware story — design + Three.js prototype.” | Make the artifact easy to remember and revisit. |

## Recording notes

- Move the pointer slowly; one intentional interaction reads better than repeated swipes.
- Let each keyframe settle before continuing so the staggered UI is visible.
- Keep the material editor closed—it is intentionally excluded from the proposal branch.
- Capture system audio only if the launch film's sound materially improves the walkthrough.
- Do not describe the concept as an official Omi release or production redesign.
